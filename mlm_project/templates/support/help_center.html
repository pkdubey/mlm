Getting Started

How do I register an account?
Visit the registration page, fill in your details (username, email, phone, password), enter a sponsor's referral code if you have one, and click "Register Now". You'll receive a confirmation email.
What is a referral code?
A referral code is a unique code assigned to each user. When someone registers using your code, they become part of your team and you earn commissions from their activities.
Is there any joining fee?
No, registration is completely free. You can start building your team immediately after signing up.

Wallets & Payments

What are the different wallet types?
We have 11 wallet types: Income, Self Income, Booster, Star, Trading Level, Salary, Reward, Growth, Sponsor Growth, USDT, and Topup wallets. Each serves a specific purpose in the MLM system.
How do I withdraw my earnings?
Go to Fund Management > Withdraw, enter the amount, select your payment method, and submit. Withdrawals are processed within 24-48 hours.
Can I transfer funds between wallets?
Yes, you can transfer funds between certain wallets. Go to Wallets > Transfer, select source and destination wallets, enter amount, and confirm.

Income & Commissions

How does referral income work?
You earn 10% direct commission from your direct referrals and level-based commissions (5% Level 1, 3% Level 2, 2% Level 3, 1% Level 4-7, 0.5% Level 8-10) from your team's activities.
When do I receive my commissions?
Commissions are credited to your wallet instantly when your team members make qualifying transactions.
